# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation. This collection of documents provides a comprehensive guide to how OctoAcme runs projects, from initial concept through successful delivery and continuous improvement.

## Overview

OctoAcme's project management approach is built on principles of customer-first thinking, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Our lightweight project lifecycle guides teams through five key phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Retrospective**. Each phase has defined goals, deliverables, and quality gates to ensure consistent, high-quality outcomes across all projects.

Our framework defines clear roles and responsibilities for Project Managers (coordinating delivery and managing risks), Product Managers (defining outcomes and prioritizing work), Developers (implementing solutions), QA/Testing (validating quality), and Stakeholders (providing guidance and approvals). This role clarity, combined with regular communication cadences and transparent tracking, enables teams to deliver customer value efficiently while managing complexity and dependencies.

Quality and continuous improvement are embedded throughout our process. From acceptance criteria and Definition of Done in planning, through automated testing and code review in execution, to structured retrospectives after each milestone, we systematically identify opportunities to improve. Our risk management and communication practices ensure stakeholders stay informed and issues are escalated appropriately, while our release and deployment standards minimize production risks and enable rapid rollback when needed.

Whether you're new to OctoAcme or looking to refresh your understanding of our project management practices, these documents provide the guidance you need to contribute effectively to project success.
