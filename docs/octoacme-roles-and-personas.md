# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers are responsible for creating user-centered experiences. They research user needs, design interfaces and interactions, and validate that solutions are intuitive and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs
- Develop and maintain design systems and style guides
- Collaborate with product and engineering to ensure designs are feasible and align with user needs
- Advocate for accessibility and inclusive design practices

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction scores
- Ensure design consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- Usability test findings and user research reports
- Design specs and handoff documentation for developers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to understand user problems and validate solutions through research
- Partners with Developers to ensure designs are technically feasible and to clarify implementation details
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align design milestones with project timelines
- Collaborates with Data Analysts to understand user behavior and validate design decisions with metrics

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts collect, process, and analyze data to inform decision-making. They build dashboards, provide insights on product metrics, and help teams measure success.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Build and maintain dashboards and reports
- Perform exploratory data analysis to identify trends and insights
- Support A/B testing and experiment analysis
- Ensure data quality and documentation

### Goals
- Provide actionable insights that drive product decisions
- Improve data-driven culture and metric literacy across teams
- Ensure accurate, timely reporting on success metrics

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metric reviews and dashboard updates
- Experiment results and statistical analysis reports
- Data requirements and schema documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define success metrics and analyze feature performance
- Partners with Developers to implement tracking and instrumentation
- Coordinates with Project Managers to report on project progress and impact metrics
- Collaborates with UX Designers to provide behavioral data and validate design hypotheses
- Supports DevOps Engineers with monitoring and performance analytics

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain the infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and operational tools that enable reliable, automated software delivery. They bridge development and operations to improve deployment speed and system reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure as code and cloud resources
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting systems
- Ensure security best practices in deployment and infrastructure
- Support incident response and root cause analysis
- Optimize build times and deployment processes

### Goals
- Minimize deployment time and manual intervention
- Maximize system uptime and reliability
- Improve observability and incident response time

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure change notifications and deployment announcements
- Incident reports and postmortems
- Performance and reliability metrics

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to understand scalability and reliability requirements
- Partners with Developers to optimize build processes and troubleshoot production issues
- Coordinates with Project Managers to plan infrastructure changes and communicate maintenance windows
- Collaborates with Data Analysts to implement monitoring and analytics infrastructure
- Supports release processes by automating deployments and ensuring rollback capabilities
